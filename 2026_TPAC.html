Mission & Core Purpose

Why an Equity Maturity Model?

Evaluative Framework

Provides W3C working groups, public sector organizations, and enterprise teams a structured mechanism to evaluate web initiatives for equity considerations.

Avenues for Maturity

Establishes actionable pathways to advance organizational maturity across policies, business operations, and organizational culture.

Shift-Left Methodology

Moves beyond post-hoc compliance audits toward preventing systemic harms, barriers, and inequities at the architectural phase.

Methodology Lifecycle

The Assessment Process: Phases 1 & 2

Phase 1

Select Program & Define Team

  • Identify criteria-based program selection (urgency, impact).
  • Stand up multidisciplinary, inclusive team including Citizen Experts.
  • Proactively log and mitigate historical/community engagement risks.
Phase 2

Define Equity Foundations

  • Document core outcomes, theory of change, and target populations.
  • Define Equity for the specific context (Procedural, Access, Outcome).
  • Co-create Personas and Journey Maps with lived-experience proxies.
W3C Draft Equity Maturity Model (EMM) Slide 8 of 12
Methodology Lifecycle

The Assessment Process: Phases 3 & 4

Phase 3

Determine Inequity & Barriers

  • Conduct quantitative analysis to highlight lifecycle disparities.
  • Conduct qualitative analysis (Service Blueprints) to identify root barriers.
  • Expose structural misalignment in policy and technical delivery.
Phase 4

Findings & Resolution Plan

  • Codify observations into preliminary insights without bias.
  • Verify findings directly with impacted Citizen Experts.
  • Co-design actionable Resolution Plan with budget and policy steps.

Community Co-Design

Centering Lived Experience (Lundy Model)

1. Space

Impacted people must be given safe, inclusive opportunities to form and express views.

2. Voice

Impacted people must be facilitated and supported to express their views freely.

3. Audience

The view must be listened to by those with decision-making power.

4. Influence

The view must be acted upon appropriately, with feedback provided on decisions.

Model Structure

EMM Dimensions & Maturity Stages

7 Organizational Dimensions

  1. Communications. Inclusive framing & accessible channels
  2. Knowledge & Skills. Workforce capacity & role training
  3. Support. Accommodations & user support systems
  4. ICT Dev Lifecycle. Design, code, & release gates
  5. Personnel. Hiring, recruiting, & ERG involvement
  6. Procurement. Vendor standards & contract language
  7. Culture. Values, leadership, & organizational mindsets

4 Cumulative Maturity Stages

  1. Inactive. No recognition or active engagement
  2. Launch. Recognized need; ad-hoc or siloed planning initiated
  3. Integrate. Structured roadmap & repeatable processes active
  4. Optimize. Fully embedded, continuously evaluated & refined
